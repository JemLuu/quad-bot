# Goal interview — question bank

Ask **one group at a time**, conversationally. Skip what the user already volunteered. Map answers to the matching sections of `athlete/profile.md`. The aim is enough to build a sensible first plan — don't interrogate.

## Group 1 — The goal (start here)
- What are you training for? A specific race (which one, what distance, what date) or a general goal (consistency, fitness, a distance milestone)?
- If a race: do you have a time goal, or is finishing/enjoying it the aim?
- Why does this goal matter to you right now? *(motivation — worth remembering)*

## Group 2 — Experience & current fitness
- How long have you been running, and how would you describe your level?
- What does a typical week look like lately — how many runs and roughly how much distance/time?
- Any recent race or hard effort that shows your current fitness?

## Group 3 — Availability & constraints
- Which days of the week can you train, and how much time on each?
- Any fixed commitments, travel, or blackout periods coming up?
- Where do you run — roads, trails, treadmill, track? Gym access for strength work?

## Group 4 — Body & history *(light touch)*
- Any current aches, niggles, or injuries I should plan around? *(If yes, suggest `/physical-therapist` for a proper look.)*
- Anything that has reliably caused you trouble when you ramp up?

## Group 5 — Preferences & units
- Miles or kilometers? *(sets `units:` in profile.md)*
- Preferred day for your long run?
- Do you prefer more hard workouts or mostly easy running?
- Anything else you'd want a coach to always keep in mind?

## After the interview
Reflect a one-paragraph summary of their goal and constraints back to them, confirm it's right, then write `profile.md`.
